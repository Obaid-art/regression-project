Machine Learning: Regression

Regression Workshop

Khalifa University  ·  Department of Chemical Engineering  ·  Prof. Ali Elkamel

One piece of equipment, thirty-one measurements. Clean the data, split it, fit a linear regression, train a neural network, and decide which one you would trust in the plant. Ninety minutes. Nothing to install, no sign-in.

Step 0

Which group are you?

Eleven rigs, one per group of three. Start with the kettle, the practice run with a coach at every step. Then pick your group. Some questions have no right or wrong answer: answer from your own understanding. The time you complete each step goes into your report.

The neural network: experiment
